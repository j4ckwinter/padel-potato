## Decisions Made

- Preserved the archive's actual Avatar child order (`56/online`, `48/offline`, `48/away`, `40/online`, `32/online`) instead of sorting the tuples into the order used by prose examples.

- Kept original `Property 1` Player Preferences metadata inspectable and normalized only the two explicitly approved values.
